# iPad support (Phase 4)

> Verbatim from Trevor's original spec (TASK.md, Addendum 2, Nov 4 3:39 AM). Do not edit; put design notes in PLAN.md.

The current iOS app does not support iPad. Plan making it a universal iPhone and iPad app: build and device settings (for example supportsTablet or device family in the app config), layouts for larger screens and both orientations (consider a split view with the chat list beside the chat), keyboard and pointer support, multitasking (Split View or Slide Over), checks across the call and dictation features, and App Store and TestFlight implications.
